Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Figra (the “Add-in”) collects and uses information.

Registration and purpose

Figra requires account registration to support ongoing development and research activities. The information you provide is collected as part of the development outcomes of this project and is used to better understand how Figra is used in educational and research contexts.

We may reference aggregated and anonymized information to improve documentation, educational materials, and future features.

As the Figra user community grows, we may offer an optional mailing list or community channel for researchers and educators to share updates, research-related information, and best practices. Participation in such communications will always be optional.

Information we collect

When you register, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Job title or role
  • Affiliation or organization
  • Country

Figra operates entirely in your browser. We do not collect usage data, error logs, or any information about your spreadsheet content.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and maintain the Add-in
  • To send service-related messages and product updates
  • To improve features, usability, and reliability
  • To comply with legal obligations

Updates and communications

We may send product updates, announcements, or information related to Figra development. In the future, these communications may include information about related tools, events, or services relevant to research and educational communities.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your information is used solely for development, research insights, and improvement of Figra.

Information may only be disclosed if required to comply with applicable laws or legal obligations.

Data retention

Registration information is retained indefinitely unless you request deletion.

Your choices

  • You may request deletion of your registration information by contacting us via the Support page.
  • You may opt out of optional communications (for example, community mailing lists) where applicable.

Contact

For privacy-related requests or questions, please contact us via the Support page: Support
