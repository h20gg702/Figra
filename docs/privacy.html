Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FigDR (the “Add-in”) collects and uses information.

Registration and purpose

FigDR requires account registration to support ongoing development and research activities. The information you provide is collected as part of the development outcomes of this project and is used to better understand how FigDR is used in educational and research contexts.

We may reference aggregated and anonymized information to improve documentation, educational materials, and future features.

As the FigDR user community grows, we may offer an optional mailing list or community channel for researchers and educators to share updates, research-related information, and best practices. Participation in such communications will always be optional.

Information we collect

When you register, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Country
  • Job title or role

We may also collect limited usage and diagnostic data (such as error logs and feature usage) to maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Add-in.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and maintain the Add-in
  • To send service-related messages and product updates
  • To improve features, usability, and reliability
  • To comply with legal obligations

Updates and communications

We may send product updates, announcements, or information related to FigDR development. In the future, these communications may include information about related tools, events, or services relevant to research and educational communities.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Add-in (for example, hosting, analytics, and email delivery), under contractual obligations to protect your data. We may also share information if required by law.

Data retention

We retain account information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Add-in. We may retain certain records longer where required for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Your choices

  • You may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information, subject to legal requirements.
  • You may opt out of optional communications (for example, community mailing lists) where applicable.

Contact

For privacy-related requests or questions, please contact us via the Support page: Support
