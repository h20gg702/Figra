Figra

Figra is an Excel Office Add-in designed to streamline figure creation from spreadsheet data. It is being developed with research and educational workflows in mind.

Quick overview

  • Create figures from Excel data with a simple task-pane workflow
  • Designed for reproducible, research-friendly usage
  • Ongoing development informed by user feedback from education and research settings

Screenshot: coming soon.

How to use

  1. Install the add-in
  2. Create an account (required) and open the task pane
  3. Generate and export figures from your data
