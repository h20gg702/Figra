Auto mode (unpaired):
1. Tests data normality (Shapiro-Wilk test)
2. Selects appropriate test based on results:
Normal data: t-test (2 groups) or ANOVA (3+ groups)
Non-normal: Wilcoxon (2 groups) or Kruskal-Wallis (3+ groups)
3. Variance test: Levene test (for t-test equal/unequal variance)
4. Post-hoc: ANOVA then Tukey, Kruskal-Wallis then Dunn
1. Tests normality of differences (2 groups) or each group (3+)
